Quick summary. Chess King uses account and game information to provide profiles, online matches, rankings, friends, and chat. The app does not currently include an advertising SDK, dedicated analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service. You can request account deletion in the app or by contacting support.

01 Who we are

Chess King is a mobile chess application published and operated by Green Store LLC (“Green Store,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). The app includes offline play, computer opponents powered by Stockfish, puzzles, online matches, player profiles, friends, ratings and rankings, and in-match chat.

This policy applies to Chess King and its related online features. It does not cover third-party services that have their own privacy policies.

03 How we use information

  • Create and authenticate accounts, including Google, Facebook, email, and guest access.
  • Provide player profiles, friends, matchmaking, online games, chat, ratings, rankings, and statistics.
  • Save preferences and progress on your device, and support troubleshooting and user requests.
  • Protect the service, enforce fair-play rules, investigate abuse, and maintain the integrity of matches.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

Chess analysis and computer play provided by the Stockfish engine run on your device. The app does not send your local analysis to a Chess King analytics service.

04 What other players can see

Online play requires some information to be visible to other players. Depending on the feature, this can include your username, Player ID, avatar, country or region setting, rating, public profile details, rankings, match result, moves, and any social links you choose to add. Text chat is visible to participants in that match.

Only add profile or chat information that you are comfortable sharing with other players. Social links may open third-party websites.

05 Device permissions and local media

  • Photo picker: You may choose an image from your gallery to preview in the profile-photo screen. In the current app version reviewed, the selected photo is not uploaded to Green Store servers or saved as your account profile image. You can also choose from the in-app avatar collection.
  • Microphone: If you choose the voice-recording control in online chat, the app records audio on your device for local playback in that chat session. The current implementation does not upload voice recordings to our servers. You can decline microphone access and continue using other app features.
  • Device authentication: Before opening the account-deletion confirmation screen, the app may ask your device to authenticate with biometrics or its passcode. Authentication is handled by your device operating system. Chess King receives the success/failure result; it does not receive or store your biometric data or passcode.
  • Local files and preferences: Some preferences, wallpaper selections, cached profile information, puzzle progress, and local match information are stored on the device.

06 Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share or allow processing only as needed to provide the features you choose, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions support account sign-in, profiles, games, and server-side operations.
  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login: If you choose either method, Google or Meta processes information under its own policies to authenticate you. Facebook login is configured not to request your email as an app permission; the provider may process technical identifiers needed for sign-in.
  • Other players: See the profile and match information described above.
  • Legal and safety: We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our rights.

We do not currently integrate in-app advertising, Firebase Analytics, a dedicated analytics SDK, or a dedicated crash-reporting SDK. Google Play may process app distribution information under its own policies.

07 Retention and account deletion

We keep account and profile information while an account is active and for as long as needed to operate the features described in this policy, address security issues, and comply with applicable law.

Text chat is stored with an online game so it can be delivered to participants. The app attempts to remove match chat when the match ends. Chat associated with a match that has not ended may remain until cleanup occurs.

Account-deletion details: Deleting an account removes the Firebase Authentication account and triggers server-side cleanup of the player profile and linked records such as friend connections, requests, challenges, likes, the Player ID mapping, and matchmaking requests. In the current implementation reviewed, deletion does not automatically remove every shared game document or its move history; those records may retain a Chess King Player ID and gameplay information. Some device-stored history or preferences may also remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Contact support to request review of a specific retained record.

Firebase or other providers may retain limited copies in backups or security logs under their own retention processes, or where retention is required by law. We do not currently publish a fixed automatic expiry period for shared match records.

To request deletion inside the app, open your profile, choose Edit Profile, scroll to Account Settings, select Delete Account, review the warning, and confirm. If you cannot sign in, use the external request option on our account deletion page.

08 Security

We use Firebase and its access controls to support account and online features, and take reasonable steps to limit access to information. Data sent to Firebase services is transmitted through their protected connections. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so keep your sign-in credentials private and do not share passwords or verification codes in chat.

09 Children’s privacy

Chess King is a general chess app and does not ask users to provide their age or date of birth. Account, profile, online-game, and chat features can involve personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of online features and should contact us if they believe a child has provided information in a way that needs review. We will handle such requests under the laws that apply to the user and the service.

Where a local law requires parental consent before information is collected from a child, the app should not be used for those online features until the required consent has been obtained.

10 Your choices and privacy rights

You can update some profile details and social links in the app, choose whether to provide optional profile or microphone permissions, and stop using online features. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to receive a copy of information.

Contact us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. If you request account deletion, see the retention details above for shared match records and information stored on your device.

11 International transfers

Green Store LLC and the service providers used by Chess King may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, transfers are handled using safeguards available under applicable law and the service provider's terms.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. We will update the date at the top of this page. If a change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required by law.
