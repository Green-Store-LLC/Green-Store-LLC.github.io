Important: Account deletion is permanent. It removes your sign-in account and triggers cleanup of account-linked profile and social records. Some shared match records and device-stored data may remain, as explained below.

01 Delete your account in Chess King

Use these steps while signed in to Chess King:

1

Open your profile

Go to your player profile and tap the Edit Profile (pencil) control.

2

Open Account Settings

Scroll to the bottom of the profile setup screen and choose Account Settings.

3

Choose Delete Account

Tap the Delete Account option. Your device will ask you to verify using biometrics or your device passcode/PIN before opening the warning screen.

4

Review and confirm

Read the warning. A 10-second countdown must finish before the permanent deletion button becomes available. Tap it to delete the Firebase Authentication account. In some cases Firebase may require you to sign in again first.

If device authentication is unsupported or you cannot sign in, request deletion by email below.

02 Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can initiate an account-deletion request by emailing Green Store LLC. Include the email address used to sign in or your Chess King Player ID so we can identify the account. We may ask for information needed to verify that you own the account. Never send your password, verification code, or payment details.

03 What account deletion removes

After a successful in-app deletion, Firebase Authentication removes the sign-in account and a server-side cleanup runs for the player profile and associated records, including the Player ID mapping, friend connections and requests, challenges, profile likes, matchmaking requests, and account-scoped profile cache.

Online match chat is intended to be removed when its match ends. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription; however, in the app version reviewed, in-app purchase and subscription flows are not enabled. If a subscription is later enabled, manage it separately through Google Play.

04 Information that may remain

Shared game records: The current account-deletion implementation does not automatically remove every shared game document or move-history subcollection. Those records may continue to contain a Chess King Player ID and gameplay information. Chat associated with a match that has not ended may also remain until cleanup occurs. Contact support to request a review of a specific record.

Some local match history, puzzle progress, preferences, or other app data may remain on your device after account deletion. To remove app data from the device, use the device’s app-storage controls or uninstall Chess King.

Copies in service-provider backups or security logs may persist temporarily under provider retention schedules or where retention is required by law. More information is in the Privacy Policy.

05 Need help?

For help with a deletion request or a record that remains after your account is deleted, contact Chess King Support. Include only information needed to identify your account or request.
